# ThriveFantasy

> ThriveFantasy was a New York City daily fantasy sports platform focused on player-prop
> pick'em contests across NFL, NBA, MLB, esports, and other leagues, backed by Bullpen
> Capital. The company ceased operations in early 2024 after a failed funding round and a
> player-withdrawal crisis. It never published a public API, developer portal, or SDKs;
> its marketing site remains online.
